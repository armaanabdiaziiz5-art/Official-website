Who We Are

Patient-centred care, built in Somalia for Somalis

Hodan Hospital is a private health care provider incorporated and registered in Somalia, offering both in-patient and out-patient care. Our aim is to offer standardized, quality and affordable care, combining innovation with compassion for the well-being of our community.

Founded by a group of Somali diaspora health professionals and inaugurated by H.E. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, the hospital is equipped to perform a wide range of surgical operations, reducing the need for medical travel abroad.

  • 24-hour emergency room
  • ICU, NICU & nursery
  • Modern operating theatres
  • Laboratory & imaging
  • In-house pharmacy
  • Ambulance service
