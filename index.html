Who We Are

Patient-centred care, built in Somalia for Somalis

Hodan Hospital is a healthcare service provider incorporated and registered in Somalia to offer affordable inpatient and outpatient healthcare services. Headquartered in Hodan District, Mogadishu, its focus is standardized, quality and affordable healthcare for communities in Somalia and beyond.

The hospital is operated by experienced medical practitioners and management staff, with an emphasis on internationally accepted healthcare standards, disease management protocols and modern information technology.

  • Emergency Room
  • ICU, NICU and Nursery
  • Operation & Anesthesia Unit
  • Laboratory Department
  • Medical Imaging Department
  • Pharmacy Services Department
