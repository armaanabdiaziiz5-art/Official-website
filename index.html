Who We Are

Patient-centred care, built in Somalia for Somalis

Hodan Hospital is a private health care provider incorporated and registered in Somalia, offering both in-patient and out-patient care. Our aim is to offer standardized, quality and affordable care, combining innovation with compassion for the well-being of our community.

Founded by a group of Somali diaspora health professionals and inaugurated by H.E. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud in February 2025, the hospital occupies a modern building of over 5,000 m² in Mogadishu's central business district, with 200 beds and four operating theatres.

  • 24-hour emergency room
  • ICU, NICU & nursery
  • Four modern operating theatres
  • Cath lab & cardiac imaging
  • Laboratory & imaging
  • In-house pharmacy
